Notes — Privacy Policy

BrightIdeas Studio · Last updated 16 July 2026

Notes is a simple to‑do / notes app for Android, Wear OS and desktop. In short: it stores only the notes you write, never asks for your identity, and shows no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

  • Your notes — the text of each note, whether it's checked off, and an optional due date you set. That is the only content the app stores.
  • Notes are saved locally on your device so the app works offline.

Cloud sync

So the same list appears on your phone, watch and computer, the app can copy your notes to a private database hosted on Supabase (a cloud database provider) and read them back. Your notes are stored under a random identifier built into the app — they are not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account, and the app never asks you to sign in.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.

Sharing

Your notes are not sold or shared with anyone. The only third party involved is the cloud database provider (Supabase) that stores the synced copy so sync can work.

Keeping and deleting your data

Notes you create stay until you delete them. Deleting a note removes it from that device and, on the next sync, from the cloud copy too. Uninstalling the app removes the notes stored on that device. To request deletion of the synced copy, email the address below.

Children

Notes is a general‑audience productivity app and is not directed at children.

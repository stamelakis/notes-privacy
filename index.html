Notes — Privacy Policy

BrightIdeas Studio · Last updated 13 September 2026

Notes is a to‑do, lists and notes app for Android, Wear OS and desktop. In short: you sign in with an email address so your notes follow you between your own devices, only you can read them, and there are no ads and no tracking.

Your account

To use the app you create an account with an email address, a password and a username. The email address is how you sign in and how a forgotten password is reset. The username is how other people can share a list with you — it is the only thing about you that anyone else can see, and nobody is ever shown your email address.

Accounts are handled by Supabase, the cloud provider that hosts the database. Your password is stored only as a cryptographic hash; nobody, including us, can read it.

What the app stores

  • Your notes and lists — to‑dos, shopping lists, project notes, routines, birthdays and namedays, films, and free‑form notes, along with whatever you mark as done and any dates or reminder times you set.
  • Your account details — email address, username, and which language you chose for the app.
  • Everything is also saved on your device, so the app works offline.

Who can read your notes

Your notes are stored against your account, and the database itself enforces that only your account can reach them. Signing in is required to see anything at all. Somebody else creating an account of their own sees their own empty lists, never yours.

The vault is stronger still: it is encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know, before it is stored or synced. Nobody can read it without that passphrase — not us, and not the cloud provider. If you forget both the passphrase and the one‑time recovery code shown when you created it, its contents cannot be recovered by anyone.

Sharing a list with someone

You can choose to share one of your lists with another person by typing their username. Only then can they read and edit that one list, and only that list. You can stop sharing at any time, and they can hand it back at any time. Nothing is shared unless you ask for it, and the vault can never be shared.

Looking up a film

On the Movies list, what you type in the search box is sent to Wikipedia so the app can offer matching films and show the year and director. That happens only while you are typing on that screen. Nothing else is sent — not your account, not your name, and none of your other lists.

What the app does not collect

  • No phone number, no contacts, no photos.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.

Who else is involved

Your notes are never sold, and never shared with anyone except the people you choose to share a list with. The only companies involved are Supabase, which hosts the database and the sign‑in system, and Wikipedia, which answers film searches as described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

Notes stay until you delete them. Deleting one removes it from that device and, on the next sync, from the stored copy too.

You can delete your entire account from inside the app — Account → Delete account. That removes your notes, your lists, your vault and the account itself. It cannot be undone and there is no copy kept to restore from. You can also email the address below to ask for the same thing.

Children

Notes is a general‑audience productivity app and is not directed at children.
